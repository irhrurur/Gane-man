VEILBREAK — IRON DISTRICT
========================
This is the updated game, not a Git patch or Windows installer.

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open Veilbreak-Phone.html with your web browser, not a text/preview app.
3. Rotate your phone sideways.
4. Choose Training Grounds > Enter the Range > Deploy Now for a quick test,
   or choose Campaign for mission objectives.

No Node installation, server, account or internet connection is required to
run this HTML. WebGL2 support is required. Some mobile file viewers refuse to
execute HTML; use the browser that successfully opened your previous version.

Left stick moves; right-side drag looks. Hold FIRE/USE; tap AIM/CROUCH,
RELOAD/JUMP/SWITCH/ABILITY/FRAG. Push the move stick farther up to sprint.
Desktop: WASD/mouse; R reload; E use; Shift sprint; C crouch; Space jump;
1/2 switch; Escape pause.

This build adds armored human infantry and a textured 160m industrial compound
with five walk-in objective rooms. It remains a prototype, not BO3 fidelity.
Phone controls and offline startup were tested in Chromium touch emulation;
physical-phone FPS has not been measured. Hand grips still need refinement.
